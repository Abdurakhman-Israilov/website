Before your first class

I have never done yoga. Which class should I pick?

Any class marked “Beginner friendly”. Arrive ten minutes early and tell the teacher it is your first time.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and water. Mats, blocks, straps and towels are free. Reformer classes need grip socks; we sell them at the desk for £8.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 6 hours before the class. Later cancellations use one class from your pass.

Is there parking?

Two free spaces behind the building, and the tram stop is a three-minute walk.
